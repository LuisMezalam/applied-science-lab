# Content And Source Policy

Applied Science Lab can use textbooks, standards, notes, and papers as learning references, but public-facing content must be original, paraphrased, and independently checked.

## Rules For Public Content

1. Do not copy textbook prose, examples, tables, figures, or solution-manual derivations into the app.
2. Write Library entries in original language focused on the unified intensity-field framing.
3. Equations may be included when they are standard mathematical/engineering relationships, but explanatory text must be newly authored.
4. When a concept is informed by a source, cite the source at a high level in internal notes or docs rather than reproducing its presentation.
5. Do not include instructor-solution content, worked answer keys, or problem statements in public UI.
6. Mark uncertain formulas or sign conventions as "needs QA" until reviewed against multiple references or a trusted derivation.

## Formula QA Checklist

Every public Library or simulator concept should have:

- Intensity field definition over a domain.
- Resultant equation.
- Centroid or center equation.
- Spread/second central moment equation.
- Negative-order or localization counterpart when meaningful.
- Units for intensity, resultant, centroid, spread, and inverse metric.
- Sign policy for nonnegative, signed, vector, or complex-valued fields.
- Domain-specific interpretation.
